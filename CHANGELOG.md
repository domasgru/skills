# Changelog

## dev-workflow

- The specify, plan, architect, implement loop and the `how` and `why` skills, moved from `filesapp-apple` at `6f09d04` and made topic-agnostic.

- Vendored `codebase-design`, `tdd` and `research` from `mattpocock/skills`.
