# Third-party skills

The plugins bundle skills vendored from the packs below with the `skills` CLI. Each copy lives in `plugins/<plugin>/.claude/skills/<skill>/`, unedited, and `plugins/<plugin>/skills-lock.json` records its upstream path and content hash. The commit is upstream `HEAD` when the copies were taken on 2026-10-08.
